# User Scenarios

## Scenario 1 (Sarah - HR Manager):
It's Monday morning, and three new software engineers just joined the company. Sarah logs into the HRMS admin panel using her credentials. She adds their profiles to the system, assigning their official starting joining dates. She knows they will automatically be able to log into the portal using their joining dates as initial temporary passwords while keeping standard security boundaries intact.

## Scenario 2 (Rahim - Employee):
Rahim's first day starts. He receives his corporate email address and his official joining date (`2026-10-08`). He opens the HRMS web app, inputs his credentials, and successfully authenticates on his first try without needing to call IT for a password reset. He lands directly on his employee dashboard to view his profile information.
