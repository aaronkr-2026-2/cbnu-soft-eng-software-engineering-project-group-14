# Proto-Personas

## 1. Primary User: HR Manager (Admin)
* **Personal:** Sarah, 32, HR Operations Lead at a growing tech firm. Spends her day handling onboarding and attendance tracking.
* **Job:** Manages employee profiles, sets up joining dates, reviews leave requests, and runs payroll reports.
* **Education:** Business Administration degree; comfortable with web dashboards but dislikes clunky, slow software.
* **Relevance:** She needs a fast, secure tool to log in, review staff records, and manage permissions without getting bogged down by database configuration.

## 2. Secondary User: Software Engineer (Employee)
* **Personal:** Rahim, 26, junior developer working remotely and on-site. 
* **Job:** Writes code, logs daily attendance, checks pay slips, and applies for leave when needed.
* **Education:** Computer Science degree; highly technical and expects clean UI/UX.
* **Relevance:** He needs a frictionless way to sign into his employee portal using his default joining date on day one and update his profile details safely.
